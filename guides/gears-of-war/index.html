Games / Gears of War

Meet Delta Squad. Choose your first fight.

Gears works best when you care about the squad. Its original trilogy, later generation, and prequels offer different beginnings without requiring novels or competitive play.

Our starting recommendation: Start the first Gears of War campaign, using Reloaded if it suits your platform. Gears 4 is a workable fresh-generation alternative. E-Day is a new origin story, currently in early access ahead of its 6 October 2026 general launch.

Choose your way in.

I want the squad’s original journey

Gears of War / Reloaded

A rescue and a clear military objective introduce Marcus, Dom, and their world through action. Reloaded retells this opening story.

Know this much
Human survivors are fighting the Locust on Sera. Marcus is returning to military service.
You can skip
Novels, comics, multiplayer, Judgment, and the prequels.
The trade-off
Later parts of the original trilogy have different platform availability. Check access before planning a three-game run.
Go next
Play Gears 2, then Gears 3. Add prequels when you want more about the squad.

I want a more modern fresh-generation start

Gears of War 4

JD, Kait, and Del introduce a new threat after the original war. Older characters provide context without replacing this group’s story.

Know this much
The old war ended; new settlements and political restrictions shape life on Sera.
You can skip
Full original-trilogy playthroughs and all external media.
The trade-off
Marcus’s personal history has more weight if you know the earlier games. The new arc continues into Gears 5.
Go next
Use the optional trilogy recap, then play Gears 5.

I want to see the invasion’s beginning

Gears of War: E-Day

Its prequel premise introduces a younger Marcus and Dom at the point their world changes. No later campaign is needed to understand that setup.

Know this much
Emergence Day is the first large-scale Locust attack on Sera.
You can skip
Every previous game and the novels.
The trade-off
As reviewed on 3 October 2026, early access is available but general launch is 6 October. This recommendation is based on the verified premise, not an assumed ending.
Go next
Continue to Gears of War for the established later squad story when ready.

Enough to begin.

Sera

The setting is Sera, a human world devastated by war; it is not Earth.

Emergence Day

The Locust emerge from underground and overwhelm human settlements. The first game begins years into the war.

Campaign first

Co-op can make the squad experience more immediate. Horde, competitive modes, collectibles, and books are optional.
