Games / Halo

Start with a ring and a mystery.

Halo’s campaigns can introduce the world through play. You do not need the novels or multiplayer ranks to understand the soldier, the AI, and the battles ahead.

Our starting recommendation: Begin with Combat Evolved, through The Master Chief Collection or the Campaign Evolved remake. Follow Halo 2 and 3 before exploring prequels. Infinite is an alternative if modern movement matters most.

Choose your way in.

I want the clearest beginning

Halo: Combat Evolved / Campaign Evolved

The arrival at an unknown ring teaches the setting through discovery, combat, and Cortana’s perspective.

Know this much
Master Chief is a Spartan; Cortana is his AI partner; the Covenant pursues their ship.
You can skip
Novels, comics, Halo Wars, Reach, and every multiplayer mode.
The trade-off
The original campaign has older level design. The 2026 remake offers another version with new optional prequel missions.
Go next
Play Halo 2, then Halo 3. Add ODST or Reach when you want another perspective.

I want modern combat and exploration

Halo Infinite

Its campaign establishes a fresh immediate mission and a new AI partnership. The core stakes are understandable through conversations.

Know this much
Chief faces the Banished on Zeta Halo after a major defeat.
You can skip
A full six-game Chief marathon and the Forerunner novels.
The trade-off
The emotional aftermath carries more weight if you know Halo 4–5. A recap helps; Infinite leaves wider questions open.
Go next
Read the optional Halo 4–5 bridge, then follow the main campaign. Go back to Combat Evolved if its mysteries draw you in.

I want a self-contained squad tragedy

Halo: Reach

A new Spartan team and a clear planetary conflict make it approachable without knowing Chief’s later history.

Know this much
Reach is a major human military world under attack.
You can skip
All previous campaigns and external lore.
The trade-off
Chronologically earlier does not mean the best introduction to the ring’s mystery. Its ending intentionally leads into Combat Evolved.
Go next
Play Combat Evolved next, or stop after this squad’s complete story.

Enough to begin.

UNSC and Covenant

Humanity’s military faces an alien coalition with a powerful religious story about ancient technology.

Spartans and AI

Enhanced soldiers and artificial intelligences form relationships that matter beyond battlefield capability.

Forerunner remains

Ancient structures are a source of mystery. Let the first campaign explain why a ring is more than a battlefield.

The bigger picture.

UNSC

Human military institutions carry different priorities from the individual people serving them.

Covenant

Its member species and political factions do not form one permanent shared perspective.

Banished

A coalition built around Atriox’s leadership, power, and rejection of Covenant authority.

Forerunners and Flood

One is an ancient civilisation, the other a parasitic threat. Their relationship is best discovered through the campaigns.
