Games / Diablo

Enter Sanctuary. Leave the checklist behind.

Choose a campaign and a class that appeal to you. Sanctuary’s history adds flavour, but it does not require decades of loot hunting before your first adventure.

Our starting recommendation: Begin with Diablo IV’s base campaign for the modern story, then its expansions in order. Choose Diablo II: Resurrected for the classic atmosphere and build-focused game.

Choose your way in.

I want the modern story

Diablo IV base campaign

A new wanderer and a clear conflict introduce Sanctuary through people living in it. The campaign supplies the relationships its expansions need.

Know this much
Lilith has returned; humanity is caught between powerful beings with competing plans.
You can skip
Diablo I–III, their difficulty ladders, and seasonal completion.
The trade-off
Start with the base campaign if you care about the story rather than using campaign-skip options.
Go next
Play Vessel of Hatred, then Lord of Hatred. Explore endgame activities when you want more.

I want the classic dark adventure

Diablo II: Resurrected

Its journey across several regions gives a clear objective and a strong sense of a world under siege. The remaster retells Diablo II and Lord of Destruction.

Know this much
A mysterious wanderer is carrying danger east from Tristram.
You can skip
The original Diablo, unless its small-town horror appeals to you. A recap is sufficient.
The trade-off
Older combat, resource management, and skill decisions demand more patience than Diablo IV.
Go next
Finish the main campaign, then Lord of Destruction. Choose further difficulty runs for the gameplay, not new required story.

I want brisk action and an approachable campaign

Diablo III and Reaper of Souls

Readable objectives and spectacular combat make this a straightforward introduction to the conflict.

Know this much
Demons threaten Sanctuary, and a fallen star draws adventurers to Tristram.
You can skip
Diablo I–II, challenge ladders, and seasonal optimisation.
The trade-off
Its brighter presentation differs from Diablo I and IV. Reaper of Souls is the next story chapter.
Go next
Play the five-act story across the base game and expansion, then stop or enter Adventure Mode.

Enough to begin.

Sanctuary

The human world lies between the ambitions of Heaven and Hell. Neither side’s claim to righteousness guarantees humanity’s safety.

Prime Evils

Diablo, Mephisto, and Baal are distinct demons associated with terror, hatred, and destruction.

Campaign versus endgame

A campaign teaches the world and your class. Seasonal progression, higher difficulties, and perfect equipment are separate commitments.

The bigger picture.

Horadrim

A scholarly order associated with containing demonic threats. Their history explains the soulstones without making the novels compulsory.

Heaven and Hell

Their struggle predates the games. Sanctuary’s people have interests that do not neatly match either side.

Cathedral of Light

A faith centred on Inarius. Its promises should be understood through the people who follow it.
