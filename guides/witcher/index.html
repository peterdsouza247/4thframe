Games / The Witcher

Meet Geralt. Follow the people who matter.

The Witcher games build on books and earlier adventures, but their quests introduce the people and conflicts you need. A compelling first journey is better than a reading-and-playing obligation.

Our starting recommendation: Start The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt for the most welcoming combination of character drama, exploration, and modern presentation. Use a short earlier-story recap if you want political context, then let the quests introduce the cast.

Choose your way in.

I want the strongest general starting point

The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt

The search for Ciri gives a large world a personal centre. Quests introduce Geralt’s relationships and explain the immediate stakes as you travel.

Know this much
Geralt is searching for Ciri while war and the Wild Hunt threaten the people around her.
You can skip
The first two games, the books, television seasons, and collecting every map marker.
The trade-off
Some returning relationships and political references assume a shared past. The optional recap reveals earlier game outcomes, and imported or simulated choices can affect details.
Go next
Finish the main story, then Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine. The 2026 remaster is a version of this campaign, not a fourth chapter.

I want a shorter political adventure

The Witcher 2: Assassins of Kings

Its focused chapters put alliances and consequences at the centre. It is a workable starting point for players more interested in political choices than a vast open world.

Know this much
Geralt is caught in the aftermath of attacks on northern rulers.
You can skip
The first game and the novels if you prefer to learn through this adventure.
The trade-off
Combat and navigation are less forgiving than The Witcher 3. A major route choice means one playthrough will not show every chapter.
Go next
Continue to Wild Hunt when you want a broader world and the Ciri storyline.

I want a self-contained ruler’s story

Thronebreaker: The Witcher Tales

Queen Meve’s campaign introduces the world through leadership, war, and difficult decisions. Geralt’s trilogy is not its prerequisite.

Know this much
Meve leads the northern kingdoms of Lyria and Rivia during Nilfgaard’s invasion.
You can skip
All three Geralt games, competitive Gwent, and the television series.
The trade-off
Its battles use a card system rather than the trilogy’s action combat. It is a different protagonist’s adventure.
Go next
Try Wild Hunt if you want to explore the same world through Geralt. You do not need to play multiplayer Gwent.

Professional monster hunters transformed and trained for dangerous work. Geralt’s profession does not determine whether a problem has an easy moral answer.

The bigger picture.

Northern kingdoms and Nilfgaard

War changes borders and daily life. Understanding the broad conflict is enough to begin; every royal genealogy is optional.

Mages

Political influence and persecution shape their choices. Individual sorceresses do not share one agenda.

Nonhuman communities

Elves, dwarves, and other peoples face different histories of exclusion. A faction’s stated cause does not settle the morality of its actions.

Witcher schools

Their training explains Geralt’s profession. School histories and equipment hunts are optional depth.
