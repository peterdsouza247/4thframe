Games / Assassin’s Creed

Choose a life in history. Let the conspiracy unfold.

Assassin’s Creed follows people whose private struggles become entangled with a secret war over freedom and control. The historical adventure is usually your way in; the Animus and modern-day story explain why someone is revisiting it. Choose a person or period first, then deepen the connections that interest you.

Our starting recommendation: Begin Assassin’s Creed II for Ezio’s character arc, Origins for an Egyptian open-world RPG, or Mirage for a more focused stealth adventure. Black Flag is a welcoming pirate route; Shadows offers a fresh pair of protagonists in Japan. Historical chronology is optional context, not our recommended first-play order.

Choose your way in.

I want a character I can grow with

Assassin’s Creed II

Ezio learns about the conflict as you do. His family, friendships and changing responsibilities make the larger conspiracy personal.

Know this much
A young Florentine is drawn into a violent conspiracy. The modern framing follows Desmond, who has escaped an organization using his inherited memories.
You can skip
Playing the original first if you mainly want Ezio’s life. The short films, collectibles and side games are optional.
The trade-off
II continues Desmond’s situation from the first game. You can follow Ezio without every detail, but start the 2007 game instead if the present-day mystery matters most.
Go next
Continue Brotherhood, then Revelations. III follows the modern-day story with a new historical protagonist; it is not a fourth Ezio game.

I want exploration and the Brotherhood’s beginnings

Assassin’s Creed Origins

Bayek and Aya’s personal loss gives the formation of an organization a human cause. Ancient Egypt provides a distinct setting and the RPG structure offers room to explore.

Know this much
Bayek is a protector in Egypt. He and his wife Aya pursue the people behind a devastating family loss.
You can skip
All earlier games, knowing the whole Isu history, clearing every map marker, and buying every expansion.
The trade-off
This is a larger RPG with levels and equipment. The historical story begins fresh, while Layla introduces another continuing modern-day thread.
Go next
The Hidden Ones is an optional follow-up about the organization taking shape. Choose Odyssey, then Valhalla in release order if you want more of Layla’s thread.

I want stealth in a focused city adventure

Assassin’s Creed Mirage

Basim’s apprenticeship teaches the Hidden Ones’ methods through investigations, tools and a close view of Baghdad.

Know this much
A street thief with troubling visions dreams of joining the Hidden Ones. Roshan becomes his mentor.
You can skip
Valhalla before beginning this historical coming-of-age story, the older games and a complete artifact glossary.
The trade-off
The ending gains extra meaning from Valhalla. Mirage works as an introduction to Basim, but choose Valhalla first if preserving its revelations is your priority.
Go next
Explore the optional Valley of Memory story if you want more Basim. Choose Valhalla for his later role, or II for another apprentice-to-mentor arc.

I want pirates, exploration and a flawed hero

Assassin’s Creed IV: Black Flag / Black Flag Resynced

Edward enters the conflict from the outside, making his ambitions and friendships a useful introduction to what the Brotherhood asks of a person.

Know this much
Edward Kenway wants wealth and independence in the Caribbean. His shortcuts draw him into the Assassin–Templar conflict.
You can skip
III before Edward’s adventure, playing both editions, and completing every naval activity.
The trade-off
The original contains a post-III modern framing and may reveal earlier series outcomes. Resynced reworks the experience; the modern-day release route in this guide uses the original.
Go next
Freedom Cry follows Adéwalé in a separate, optional story. Rogue and III explore the later Atlantic conflict; play III after the earlier Desmond games if following that modern-day arc.

I want a new cast and contrasting perspectives

Assassin’s Creed Shadows

Naoe and Yasuke offer different relationships to power and different ways through the same turbulent setting. You meet their local allies and opponents within the game.

Know this much
Naoe is a shinobi from Iga; Yasuke is a samurai whose path intersects hers during Japan’s wars of unification.
You can skip
Every earlier historical adventure, DLC before the base story, and knowing all previous Assassins by name.
The trade-off
Its dual protagonists and larger systems differ from Mirage’s concentrated stealth approach. Shared-world references add context but need not dictate your starting point.
Go next
Claws of Awaji is an optional continuation after the base story. Try Mirage for a smaller stealth route or Origins for another large historical world.

Enough to begin.

Two layers, two orders

The Animus lets a present-day user experience the past. Historical events jump between periods as the modern-day story moves forward. Sorting by century can therefore put later revelations before their setup. Follow release order if the present-day mystery is your main interest.

Freedom, order and imperfect people

Assassins broadly defend freedom; Templars seek order through control. Individual characters complicate that division. Notice when revenge, duty or ambition changes what a person is willing to justify. The games’ secret history is fiction.

Ancient technology, not a required mythology course

The Isu were an advanced civilization whose surviving artifacts affect human history. Pieces of Eden are powerful objects tied to that legacy. Their precise functions and the characters’ discoveries are part of the stories; learn them as they become relevant.

The bigger picture.

Assassins and the Hidden Ones

Different names mark stages of an organization dedicated to resisting domination. Its Creed asks for discipline as well as freedom. Origins explores the formation; Mirage shows an established network; Ezio shows recruitment and leadership.

Templars and precursor orders

The recurring opposing project pursues order through control. Names and institutions change with the period. The Order of the Ancients and the Cult of Kosmos should not simply be treated as identical labels for every ancient opponent.

Abstergo and the present

Abstergo gives the modern conflict a corporate face. The Animus turns inherited history into an object of research and exploitation. Follow the modern participants to understand who is looking into the past and why.

Isu and Pieces of Eden

The First Civilization’s technology provides the shared world’s deeper mystery. Powers that appear supernatural can have an ancient technological explanation within the fiction. Specific identities and artifact revelations belong to each game’s discoveries.

Local lives matter

A pirate republic, an Indigenous community, a threatened family or a divided city is more than a stage for secret orders. The historical characters’ loyalties explain why faction slogans do not settle every decision.

A manageable mythology route

For the original modern arc: 2007 → II → Brotherhood → Revelations → III. For Layla: Origins → Odyssey → Valhalla, with Fate of Atlantis as a substantial connective detour. These are alternatives to committing to the whole franchise.

Sources and scope

Reviewed 3 October 2026. Entry points, optional routes, and play order are Fourth Frame’s editorial recommendations.

Covers fourteen distinct mainline historical adventures through Shadows, Liberation and Freedom Cry, plus Black Flag Resynced as an alternative telling. Original release years distinguish remakes and remasters from new story chapters. Released additions such as The Hidden Ones, The Fate of Atlantis, Valley of Memory and Claws of Awaji are discussed as selected detours rather than exhaustively summarized. Modern-day outcomes are covered only where explicitly disclosed; Odyssey and Valhalla have orientation rather than full ending recaps. Chronicles, handheld and VR games, multiplayer projects, novels, comics, films and announced future games are outside this first guide’s full timeline. No future title’s outcomes or release dates are invented.
