Games / Mass Effect

Meet the crew. Find your starting point.

You do not need the novels, comics, multiplayer, or every side quest. Pick the kind of adventure you want, then let the crew and the main story guide you.

Our starting recommendation: Start Mass Effect 1 in Legendary Edition for the fullest character journey. If its older combat is a barrier, Mass Effect 2 is a workable alternative. Andromeda offers a separate exploration story.

Choose your way in.

I want the strongest character journey

Mass Effect 1 in Legendary Edition

You meet the crew before their relationships and choices accumulate. Legendary Edition collects the three main games and their major story DLC in one package.

Know this much
Humanity is new to galactic politics. Shepard is a soldier whose values and relationships you help define.
You can skip
Novels, comics, multiplayer, exhaustive planet surveys, and a perfect-playthrough checklist.
The trade-off
The first game’s combat and exploration show their age. Choose a comfortable difficulty and follow your curiosity rather than clearing every map.
Go next
Import your save into Mass Effect 2, then Mass Effect 3. Add the suggested DLC when it fits your interests.

I want to meet the crew with more immediate action

Mass Effect 2

Recruitment and loyalty missions provide a clear structure, and the combat is easier to enter than the original game.

Know this much
Use the included Genesis recap, where available, to establish key Mass Effect 1 choices. It cannot reproduce every relationship or decision from a full save.
You can skip
A full Mass Effect 1 playthrough if it is stopping you from beginning, plus all external media.
The trade-off
You lose the experience of meeting returning characters for the first time. Some details use defaults rather than decisions from a full imported save.
Go next
Read the ME1 recap only if you accept its spoilers, play recruitment and loyalty missions, then continue to Mass Effect 3.

I want exploration and flexible combat

Mass Effect: Andromeda

A new protagonist, crew, and galaxy provide an independent way into the setting. No trilogy save is required.

Know this much
The Initiative has travelled from the Milky Way to find new homes. You play Ryder, a Pathfinder working with explorers and settlers.
You can skip
The entire Shepard trilogy, its DLC, novels, comics, and multiplayer.
The trade-off
Its tone and structure differ from the trilogy, and it leaves some wider questions open. It is not the continuation of Shepard’s crew story.
Go next
Play the main campaign and crew missions you enjoy. Try Mass Effect 1 later if you want the trilogy’s political and character journey.

Enough to begin.

Humanity is the newcomer

Humans are still negotiating their place in a galaxy shaped by older spacefaring societies.

Relays and the Citadel

Mass relays connect distant regions. The Citadel is a political centre where species compete and cooperate.

Choices carry forward

Importing your save preserves many decisions across the trilogy. There is no need to chase a perfect outcome or consult a guide for every conversation.

The bigger picture.

Citadel Council

A governing body whose established species do not automatically share humanity’s priorities.

Systems Alliance

Humanity’s principal military and political organisation in the trilogy.

Quarians and geth

Their conflict concerns displacement, synthetic life, and competing accounts of responsibility. Learn through the games before seeking a definitive verdict.

Krogan

A people shaped by the genophage and by arguments over survival, leadership, and the future.

Cerberus

An organisation claiming to advance human interests. Treat its account of its motives as a viewpoint to question.

Andromeda Initiative

A settlement and exploration project. Its story belongs to Ryder’s branch rather than Shepard’s campaign.

No single romance, survival outcome, or ending is treated as everyone’s canon.
