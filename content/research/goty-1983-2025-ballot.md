# Fourth Frame — Game of the Year: historical decision ballot (1983–2025)

**Editorial record — all 43 Fourth Frame choices approved.** The completed retrospective uses these selections. Preserve the historical award result even when Fourth Frame disagrees.

## How to read this ballot

- **Year** is the Golden Joystick *award/ceremony year* when a result exists, not always the original launch year. For example, the Golden Joysticks recognised GTA III in 2002 and Skyrim in 2012 despite their earlier launches. Other historically significant games on the list are **retrospective editorial alternatives**, not claimed official runners-up.
- From 1988–1990 there were multiple platform winners. Some mid-1990s ceremonies were combined or the awards were on hiatus; **do not manufacture a winner**.
- The historical award of reference is the Golden Joystick, not The Game Awards (which only started in 2014). In the eventual article, show the Golden Joystick result and Peter's own pick separately; note when The Game Awards or Spike selected a different game.
- For rows with no official winner, choose a **retrospective Fourth Frame winner** from the options.
- Peter can choose any other qualifying game and add a personal reason. Update the `Fourth Frame pick` column with his choice, then rewrite each row as a concise, individual listicle entry stating what it did particularly well and why it did or did not deserve the historical award.
