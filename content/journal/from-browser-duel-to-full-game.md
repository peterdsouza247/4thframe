*Masters of the Way* already has six arts, seven AI opponents, a Gauntlet, a tutorial, a deck builder and local two-player duels. That is enough to invite people into the [browser build](https://peterdsouza247.github.io/masters-of-the-way/). It is also enough to learn which parts deserve more work.

## First, let people play

The next step is an itch.io browser playtest. I want to know where a new player loses the thread: when range changes, when a grapple reaches the floor, when they build their first deck, or when a match ends in a way they did not expect. The existing profile export should let people carry their decks and fight record between devices; its behaviour inside itch's embedded player needs checking too.

## What a desktop edition would add

A paid desktop release would have to offer more than the free browser duels. The leading idea is a career or tournament structure: opponents who become rivals, decisions between fights, and progression that changes what a deck can become. A desktop package, polished interface and focused demo would be part of that work. I do not want to put a release date on it before playtest feedback defines the scope.

## And the phone?

The browser game already accepts touch input. A Play Store edition would still need its own device testing and a mobile experience that feels at home in a short session. Extra arts and online multiplayer are possibilities, not commitments. Each would need to make the central duel more interesting enough to justify its complexity.

The [public roadmap](https://github.com/peterdsouza247/masters-of-the-way/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) records those stages. It will change as the game and its players give me better evidence.
