# Character-first watch guide: MCU + Star Wars

**Edition:** 28 September 2026 · **Scope:** released screen stories only · **Spoiler level:** premise and broad character turns, no ending reveals.

## How to use this guide

Pick a character or group and watch the **Core** titles from left to right. **Extras** are optional expansions, with a reason to choose each. A title can be core for one character and an extra for another. “Core” is an editorial judgment about following *that character's* arc, not a claim that every scene is essential to the whole franchise. Films are in release order unless a series clearly belongs between films. A season means the full season; a named episode or arc means only that selection. For an ongoing story, “complete” means the available arc so far, not a promised final ending. Parenthesized years distinguish similarly named films. Some series and ensemble paths are much longer than film-only paths.

**Scope boundaries:** MCU means the shared Marvel Studios film and connected live-action television continuity. Pre-MCU Spider-Man/X-Men films are separate continuities, added only where a crossover benefits from them. *What If…?*, *Marvel Zombies*, and *Your Friendly Neighborhood Spider-Man* are alternative-continuity extras. Star Wars means the main screen canon; *Visions*, LEGO specials, and older Legends stories are excluded. Shorts and animation are included when they carry a character's plot. Future announced titles are deliberately absent.

**Useful MCU mini-marathons:**

- **The Infinity Saga through six viewpoints:** Tony, Steve, Thor, Natasha, Wanda, and the Guardians. Use their rows to see why each crossover matters.
- **A complete short arc in one sitting:** *Moon Knight* or *Wonder Man* (series); *Shang-Chi*, *Eternals*, or *The Fantastic Four: First Steps* (films with open futures).
- **Street-level sequence:** *Daredevil* S1–2 → *The Defenders* → *Daredevil* S3 → *Hawkeye* → *Echo* → *Born Again* S1–2. Add *The Punisher* and *Jessica Jones* along their rows.
