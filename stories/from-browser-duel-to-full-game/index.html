Masters of the Way / Roadmap

From a browser duel to a full game

By Peter D’Souza
DEVELOPMENT JOURNAL
Fourth Frame
Journal / 1 min

Masters of the Way already has six arts, seven AI opponents, a Gauntlet, a tutorial, a deck builder and local two-player duels. That is enough to invite people into the browser build. It is also enough to learn which parts deserve more work.

First, let people play

The next step is an itch.io browser playtest. I want to know where a new player loses the thread: when range changes, when a grapple reaches the floor, when they build their first deck, or when a match ends in a way they did not expect.

What a desktop edition would add

A paid desktop release would have to offer more than the free browser duels. The leading idea is a career or tournament structure: opponents who become rivals, decisions between fights, and progression that changes what a deck can become. A desktop package, polished interface and a focused demo would be part of that work.

And the phone?

The browser game already accepts touch input. A Play Store edition would still need its own device testing and a mobile experience that feels at home in a short session on the phone.

The public roadmap records these stages. It is a living document and will change as I receive valuable feedback from players.
