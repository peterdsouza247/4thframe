10 Best Vampires In Gaming (And The Underrated Ones You're Sleeping On)

By Peter D’Souza
VAMPIRES
Fourth Frame
List / 01

Vampires have appeared in games as bosses, companions, monarchs and player characters. Some of the best examples make the condition change how you play, while others build an entire world around blood and inheritance. These are the ones I keep thinking about.

10. Total War: Warhammer's Vampire Counts

Total War: Warhammer gives the Vampire Counts armies instead of a handful of victims. Vlad von Carstein conquers territory and raises the dead to hold it. The familiar vampire story becomes a campaign of military expansion, with every victory adding bodies to the cause.

9. Vampire: The Masquerade – Bloodlines

Your clan choice changes the way Bloodlines plays. Malkavian dialogue takes strange turns; a Nosferatu cannot walk openly through the same social spaces as everyone else. The curse follows you into conversations and routes through the city. Picking a clan means accepting its inconveniences as well as its powers.

8. Skyrim's Vampire Lords

In Skyrim, the Vampire Lord form offers flight, life drain and the ability to raise thralls. Serana's story gives that power a less comfortable setting: her father treats her as something he owns. She has to live with a legacy she did not choose, even as the player enjoys the abilities it grants.

7. Vampyr

Vampyr makes feeding a decision with a name attached. Every person Jonathan Reid kills has relationships and a place in their district; the neighbourhood suffers when enough people disappear. Holding back costs you useful experience, so restraint asks something of the player.

6. The Blood of Dawnwalker

Coen is human by day and a vampire by night. Those two states give him different ways to move through the world while a thirty-day deadline hangs over his family. It is a premise built for uncomfortable scheduling: every detour spends time he cannot get back.

5. Elden Ring's Chanting Winged Dames

FromSoftware does not call the Chanting Winged Dames vampires. Their bat-like forms and mournful song put them close enough to the tradition for this list. The tragedy comes through their presence in the world and the words of their song, leaving the player to piece together what happened to them.

4. Bloodborne's Vilebloods

Cainhurst Castle gives Bloodborne's obsession with blood an aristocratic face. Queen Annalise and the Vilebloods have their own rites and history, apart from the Healing Church. Discovering them changes how the game's familiar blood imagery feels on a second visit.

3. Legacy of Kain

Kain and Raziel spend the Legacy of Kain series fighting over fate, history and one another. Their relationship survives betrayals and changes to the timeline. Few games have let their vampires argue for this long over whether they can escape the roles assigned to them.

2. Astarion in Baldur's Gate 3

Astarion's hunger is tied to years of control by Cazador. Helping him escape does not settle what he should do when that power becomes available to him. Baldur's Gate 3 lets the player influence the choice, but Astarion has to live with it.

1. Castlevania

Alucard faces Dracula as both an enemy and a father. That family conflict gives Castlevania more to work with than another boss fight. He has inherited a connection to the monster and decided to resist it; plenty of later vampire stories return to the same difficult choice.
